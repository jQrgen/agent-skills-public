## Event Recap

This skill helps DevRel team members document and share what happened at an event — conference, meetup, hackathon, webinar, or community call. A good recap captures the story of the event, surfaces insights for the team and community, and drives follow-up action.

### Recap Structure

When asked to write an event recap, produce a document with the following sections:

1. **Event Overview** — Name, date, location/format, and a one-paragraph summary of what the event was and why GitLab participated.
2. **GitLab Presence** — What did the team do? (talks, booth, sponsorship, workshops, hallway conversations)
3. **Key Takeaways** — 3–5 insights about the community, the market, or the technology landscape observed at the event.
4. **Talk / Session Recaps** — For each GitLab talk or session: title, speaker, summary, audience reaction, and a link to slides or recording.
5. **Community Sentiment** — What are people excited about? What are they frustrated with? What questions came up repeatedly?
6. **Competitive Intelligence** — What were other vendors talking about? Any notable announcements or positioning shifts?
7. **Metrics** — Attendance, booth traffic, talk attendance, social mentions, leads generated, or other relevant numbers.
8. **Follow-up Actions** — Specific next steps with owners and due dates.
9. **Content Opportunities** — Blog posts, videos, or social content this event suggests.
10. **What to Do Differently** — Honest retrospective: what worked, what didn't, what to change next time.

### Audience Adaptation

| Audience | Focus | Format |
|----------|-------|--------|
| Internal team | Metrics, follow-ups, retrospective | Bullet points, action items |
| Public blog | Story, takeaways, community highlights | Narrative, photos, quotes |
| Leadership | ROI, pipeline impact, strategic insights | Executive summary, numbers first |
| Community | What happened, what was shared | Warm, inclusive, links to resources |

### Key Takeaway Guidelines

A strong takeaway is:
- **Specific** — "Developers at KubeCon are frustrated with YAML-heavy CI configuration" not "people care about CI/CD"
- **Actionable** — It suggests something the team should do, build, or write
- **Sourced** — Tied to a conversation, session, or observation from the event

### Community Sentiment Guidelines

Capture sentiment across three dimensions:
- **Excitement:** What are people energized about? What demos got the best reactions?
- **Frustration:** What pain points came up repeatedly? What do people wish existed?
- **Questions:** What did people not understand or want to learn more about?

### Metrics to Track

| Metric | Source |
|--------|--------|
| Event attendance | Organizer report |
| GitLab talk attendance | Room count or organizer data |
| Booth traffic | Badge scans or manual count |
| Social mentions | Sprout Social, Brand24, or manual search |
| New community members | Discord/forum join spike |
| Content engagement | Blog views, video plays post-event |

### Output Format

Use the example in `example-event-recap.md` as a reference for tone and depth. Adapt to the specific event, audience, and data provided by the user.
