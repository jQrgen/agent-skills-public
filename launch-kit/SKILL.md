# Launch kit

1. Collect the facts: what launched, the link, the one thing a developer should take away, and the real numbers behind it with their sources.
2. Run the Public claim check on those facts first.
3. Draft:
   - a main social post (one hook sentence, the takeaway, and the link);
   - one or two reply posts (how it works, plus the paper or source link);
   - a one-line slide caption for decks;
   - a short community note for forums or chats (three or four sentences).
4. Send visuals and the page to the design owner for sign-off, and send all text to the user for approval.
5. Nothing gets posted or sent until the user approves. Deliver drafts only.
6. After launch, offer to draft answers to replies and to note how the post performed, using the numbers the platform shows.
