## Demo Planning

This skill helps DevRel team members plan compelling technical demos. A great demo tells a story, solves a real problem the audience recognizes, and recovers gracefully when things go wrong.

### Demo Plan Structure

When asked to plan a demo, produce a document with the following sections:

1. **Overview** — One-paragraph summary of what the demo shows and why it matters to the audience.
2. **Audience & Context** — Who is watching, what they care about, and what event or format this is for.
3. **Core Narrative** — The problem-solution story arc (Before / Conflict / After).
4. **Prerequisites** — Environment setup, accounts, data, or configuration needed before the demo starts.
5. **Demo Steps** — Numbered steps with:
   - Action to take
   - What to say (talking point)
   - What the audience should notice
6. **Key Moments** — 2–3 "wow" moments to emphasize and linger on.
7. **Timing** — Estimated time per section and total runtime.
8. **Fallback Plan** — What to do if a live step fails (screenshot, recording, or skip path).
9. **Call to Action** — What you want the audience to do after the demo.

### Narrative Arc Guidelines

- **Before:** Describe the painful status quo the audience lives in today.
- **Conflict:** Introduce the specific friction point the demo will resolve.
- **After:** Show the world with the solution in place — make it feel real and achievable.

Avoid feature-listing. Every step should connect back to the narrative.

### Timing Guidelines

| Format | Recommended Demo Length |
|--------|------------------------|
| Conference talk (30 min) | 8–12 minutes |
| Conference talk (45 min) | 12–18 minutes |
| Webinar / live stream | 15–25 minutes |
| Short-form video / reel | 2–5 minutes |
| Sales / partner demo | 20–30 minutes |

### Fallback Strategies

- **Screenshot fallback:** Prepare annotated screenshots of each critical step.
- **Recording fallback:** Record a clean run-through in advance; embed as a video backup.
- **Narrative fallback:** If all else fails, walk through the story verbally using slides and return to live demo for the final reveal.

### Environment Checklist

Before any live demo:

- [ ] Demo environment is isolated from production
- [ ] All required data and accounts are pre-seeded
- [ ] Browser tabs are pre-opened and logged in
- [ ] Notifications and Do Not Disturb are enabled
- [ ] Font size and zoom level are set for visibility
- [ ] Terminal history is cleared
- [ ] Fallback screenshots or recording is ready
- [ ] Demo has been rehearsed end-to-end at least twice

### Output Format

When generating a demo plan, use the example in `example-demo-plan.md` as a reference for tone, depth, and structure. Adapt the content to the specific product area, audience, and event provided by the user.
