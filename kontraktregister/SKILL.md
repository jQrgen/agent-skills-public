# Kontraktregister

One register is the single source for contract dates. Other lists (client lists, cash plans) link to entries here instead of copying dates.

## Fields per contract
- ID, counterparty (legal name, org/registration no., country)
- Type (client, supplier, subscription, lease, other) and short scope
- Signed date, start date, end date or 'løpende'
- Renewal: automatic or not, renewal period
- Notice period (oppsigelsestid) and the computed last notice date
- Price/rate and currency, payment terms (only as stated in the contract)
- Owner on the team, location of the signed PDF in the archive
- Source and status (aktiv, sagt opp, utløpt, utkast)

## Steps
1. Get the signed document from the owner via the agreed channel. Never guess terms; mark unknown fields as 'ukjent – venter på <who>'.
2. Read the contract and fill every field from the text, citing clause numbers.
3. Store the PDF in the document archive (retention per company policy, e.g. 5 years after expiry) and link it.
4. Compute next key dates: last notice date, renewal date, end date.
5. Alert the coordinator (chief of staff) about any key date within 90 days, and again at 30 days.
6. Tell dependent owners (pipeline list, CFO buffer planning) the entry ID so they link to it.
7. Review the register monthly; mark expired or terminated contracts, never delete them.

## Rules
- Never sign, renew, terminate or contact a counterparty without the owner's explicit approval.
- Keep private and company agreements strictly separate.
- Never put contract terms or amounts on public pages.
